PrivFace M2/M3 research model

Training images: Olivetti faces, AT&T Laboratories Cambridge.
400 images / 40 subjects; modified 64x64 dataset described by scikit-learn:
https://scikit-learn.org/stable/datasets/real_world.html#olivetti-faces-dataset
https://github.com/scikit-learn/scikit-learn/blob/main/sklearn/datasets/descr/olivetti_faces.rst

are a small research feasibility artifact, not a validated face-login model.
